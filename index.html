        Our story

        Made for curious snackers.

        Makhana is light, puffy and wonderfully crunchy. We roast it, toss it in flavour, and pack it into jars that look good on a desk, a shelf or a coffee table.

        Some days call for smoke and heat, some for something mellow and herby, some for a green-chilli kick. So we made three. Start with the one that sounds like you, then try the others.

        • Desk breakLid off, a handful, back to work.
        • Movie nightTip a jar into a bowl and share. Or don’t.
        • Chai timeSomething crunchy next to the cup.
